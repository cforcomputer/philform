Questions

Good to know.

Does phil submit applications for me?

No, never. phil fills the form and shows you what it did. You read it over and press submit yourself.

Which job sites does it work on?

Any application form in your browser: company careers pages and the systems behind them, such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and iCIMS. It reads each form fresh, so new and unusual forms work too.

Will the written answers sound like a robot?

They are written from your own CV and projects, one real example per question, and checked for the stock phrases that give machine writing away. Read them before you submit and change anything you like. Answers you save are reused word for word.

What if phil gets something wrong?

Everything it filled is outlined, and anything it was unsure of is outlined in amber, so you know where to look. Fix it on the page, click "Save my answers", and phil fills it your way next time.

What happens to my information?

Your profile stays in your browser. When you fill a form, the questions and the parts of your profile needed to answer them are sent to the AI that fills it. philform's service passes them on without keeping them, and we never sell data. The privacy policy has the details.

How does paying work?

Your first 10 applications are free. After that you add $5 to a wallet, and each fill takes what it used, about a cent. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Questions about a payment or a refund go to support.

Which browsers?

Firefox on a computer: download it below. Chrome is in closed beta, and other Chromium browsers, such as Edge and Brave, can install it from the Chrome Web Store once it opens.
