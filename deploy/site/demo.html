Try phil on this practice form

  1. Pin phil to your toolbar. Click the puzzle piece at the top right of your browser, then the pin next to philform.
  2. Click phil in the toolbar, then press phil this application.
  3. Check every answer. Answers outlined in amber are phil's best guess. Fix anything that is not right.
  4. Tell phil how it did. Press the thumbs up or down in the popup. A thumbs down sends this page for review, never your answers.

This is only practice: nothing you enter here is sent anywhere, and practice fills do not use your free applications. If a box stays empty, phil does not know that detail yet: add it on the Profile tab and press Save profile.
